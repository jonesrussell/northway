API keys and usage limits

Read-only is the default. Up to five active keys; each expires after 30 days. Browser and API share 60 requests per minute. Revocation stops future authenticated requests; in-flight work may finish.

    curl --fail --header "Authorization: Bearer $NORTHCLOUD_KEY" https://api.northcloud.one/v1/feeds

    Sources, privacy and support
    The initial catalogue is limited to official developer-news sources approved for this beta. No full article pages, images or private feeds are collected. Results show source and freshness information; missing coverage is not a claim that no news exists.
    Source metadata: The Go Authors and Kubernetes contributors, under CC BY 4.0. Titles may be normalized or stripped of markup. Follow each headline for the original and author notices. No publisher endorsement is implied.
    Feedback records your explicit action. It does not train or change ranking automatically. Raw query context is not stored by the Go service. Credentials are never sent to analytics.
    For account recovery, export, deletion or a source problem, contact your invitation operator. Customer activation requires tested support and recovery procedures.
