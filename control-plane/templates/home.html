Know what you’re getting

Focused by design.
Still a beta.

The current catalogue covers the official Go and Kubernetes blogs. It does not provide complete developer-news coverage.

NorthCloud works with headline metadata, not full article pages. Ranking is deterministic; feedback is recorded but does not automatically teach the ranking system.

Invited accounts can sign in immediately. Email addresses are unverified, and there is no email delivery during this beta.

Before you join

How do I get access?

Registration requires an operator-issued, single-use invitation. If you have one, use your invitation. Public signup is closed; contact the person who introduced you to NorthCloud about availability.

Do I need to share my project files?

No. A query needs only the context you choose to provide. Keep secrets, project files and whole conversations out of requests. NorthCloud does not need access to your repository.

What if I need recovery, export or deletion?

Contact the person who issued your invitation for operator-assisted account recovery, export or deletion. If you already have a recovery token, reset your password.
